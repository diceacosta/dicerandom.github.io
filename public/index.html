About Me

Hi there! I'm a passionate game developer with nearly 8 years of experience in programming and game creation. My journey started in middle school, where I discovered a love for building interactive worlds.

 

Since then, I've immersed myself in every aspect of game development, gaining invaluable hands-on knowledge that traditional learning couldn't provide.

 

Now at 20 years old, I've honed a diverse skill set that includes:

  • Programming: Building robust and scalable game systems. This is is my main focus and what drives me to make games.
  • 3D Modeling & Animation: Designing and animating assets to bring ideas to life.
  • Networking: Developing multiplayer systems for seamless player interaction.
  • UI/UX Design: Crafting user interfaces that enhance the player experience.

 

Game development has been my creative outlet and technical playground, allowing me to combine artistry and engineering. I'm always excited to tackle new challenges and push the boundaries of what's possible in interactive entertainment.
